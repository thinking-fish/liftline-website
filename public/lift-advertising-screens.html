Lift & elevator advertising

Turn captive lift time into local advertising income

Every lift journey is undivided attention — no app, nothing to scroll past. A LiftLine digital advertising screen puts that attention to work for the building, not against it.

Full-screen, brand-safe adverts

Vetted local businesses — cafés, gyms, estate agents, restaurants — run full-screen adverts curated to the neighbourhood. Always tasteful, never intrusive, and woven between genuinely useful resident content.

Questions

Lift advertising screens — the basics

What is a lift digital advertising screen?

A lift digital advertising screen (also called an elevator advertising screen) is a wall-mounted display fitted inside a building's lift car. It shows useful content for residents — news, transport, weather — alongside full-screen adverts from local businesses, turning lift journeys into advertising airtime.

How do lift advertising screens make money for a building?

LiftLine sells full-screen advertising slots to vetted local businesses and shares the revenue with the building, helping offset service charges and running costs. Each advert can carry a QR code linking residents straight to an offer.

Where can elevator advertising screens be installed?

In the lift cars of residential apartment blocks and commercial buildings. Installation is a single visit — a wall-mounted iPad is fitted and connected, with no wiring projects or disruption to residents.

Is it just adverts?

No — most of the screen time is useful resident content (news, Tube status, departures, weather, building notices). The advertising is woven in tastefully, which is what keeps residents looking and the slots valuable. See our lift digital notice board for the resident side.
