What we collect

  • Admin users: name, work email, role, and the estates you are assigned to. Held in AWS Cognito (eu-west-2).

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't share resident lists with advertisers — the adverts that play in a lift are scheduled by the building's manager, not us.
