Support

LiftLine is provided by Thinking Fish Ltd, London.

Get in touch

Email hello@liftline.co.uk or call 020 7100 2222. We answer emails within one working day. If a screen in a live building is down, say so in the subject line and we will pick it up first.

Linking a screen to your building

LiftLine needs a subscription for your building before a screen can show anything. Once you have one:

  1. Install LiftLine on the iPad and open it. It shows a six character code.
  2. On any computer, sign in to app.myliftline.com.
  3. Go to Devices → Pair a display and type in the code.

The screen picks up its building within a few seconds and starts showing your notices. Nothing is typed at the screen itself, which matters when it is fifteen feet up a lobby wall.

If a screen goes blank or says "We will be back soon"

That card means the iPad cannot reach the internet. It appears only after three failed attempts, and the screen recovers on its own as soon as the connection comes back — there is nothing to press. If it persists, check the building's wifi first, then email us with the building name and which lift it is.

Changing what is on the screen

Everything is edited at app.myliftline.com — notices, adverts, the weather location, which Tube station and bus stops to show, how long each screen stays up, and the order the screens appear in. Changes reach a running display within a few minutes; you do not need to touch the iPad.

Privacy

The display is one way. LiftLine does not use the camera or the microphone and holds no data about the people who travel in the lift. The full notice is at myliftline.com/privacy.
